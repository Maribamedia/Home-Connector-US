Strategic Memo · On Routing, Not Outreach

Most routing runs one way. We build it both ways.

The home services market does not lack demand. It does not lack supply. It lacks a routing layer that reads both at once — and matches them through research, not volume.

Contractors, restoration experts, electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians — supply sits with capacity that cannot see the bids. Vendor recruitment firms, insurance panels, general contractors, facilities managers — demand sits with projects that cannot see the compliant capacity. Most marketplaces pick a side — sell-side outbound, buy-side sourcing — and route in one direction only.

The leverage is not in outreach. It is in routing — demand to opportunity, and supply to revenue pipelines, in the same motion.

The work is research, not outreach. Who controls the decision. What their live requirement is. What compliance sits on both sides. Then matching: requirements to qualified capacity. Only then do we introduce.

Three flows — demand to opportunity, supply to revenue, bids and projects to qualified capacity — run on one routing layer, filtered the same way.

Value. Does the introduction make sense for both sides, not just ours? Demand. Is the requirement live, with a counterparty who can act? Compliant capacity. Can the supply side actually deliver — legally, operationally and at the standard the buyer expects?

When all three filters clear, we route the signal in whichever direction the demand sits. When one fails, we do not make the introduction. That discipline is the product.

We do not route once and walk away. In every vertical we operate, demand and supply signals are guaranteed to exist. If the first curated introduction does not reach fulfillment, we route again. We keep routing until fulfillment.

We are not a broker. Not a lead-gen agency. Not a job board. We are a routing layer that runs in both directions — and does not stop at the first introduction.

Being across the US means we can read demand in one region and route compliant capacity from another. Insurance panels run on the carrier's timeline. Municipal procurement runs on the tender calendar. Enterprise facilities run on the rollout schedule. The routing layer adapts; the discipline does not.

Because in some markets, one introduction is noise. In others, one introduction — routed in the right direction — changes the direction of a company.

The Routing Principle

Qualified demand in every vertical. And routing until fulfillment.

If we open a vertical, the signals are live — demand and qualified capacity, both readable. We do not promise any single introduction converts. We do promise we keep routing curated introductions until it does.

Guaranteed

Signals exist.

In every vertical we operate, live demand and qualified capacity are there. We do not open a vertical we cannot read. If we take a brief, the signals are there.

Committed

Routing until fulfillment.

If the first curated introduction does not land, we route again — same agreement, no extra fee — until the requirement is fulfilled or the brief is withdrawn.

The principle covers the routing process: signals exist in every vertical we operate, and curated introductions continue until fulfillment. Commercial outcomes remain with the counterparty.

Commercial Terms

Fixed. Transparent. Aligned.

No commission you cannot see. No retainer you did not agree to. Three engagement models, all anchored on the routing decision — and all quoted upfront.

01

Fixed Success Fee

Upon fulfillment

A fixed fee agreed in advance, triggered when the routed introduction reaches fulfillment. If the first route does not convert, we keep routing — same agreement, no extra fee — until it does. Disclosed upfront and does not move after signature.

02

Performance Retainer

Ongoing mandate

For counterparties who want a routing mandate on retainer. The retainer is credited against success fees. Available on demand, structured per vertical, reviewed at each routing window. Routing-until-fulfillment applies to every brief.

03

Partnership

Based on demand

For networks, funds and platforms that want a routing partnership across the US. Negotiated per vertical, based on the demand profile you bring and the counterparties you need. Partnerships carry the same routing-until-fulfillment commitment.

Common Questions

What decision makers ask first.

What does HomeConnector actually do?▾

HomeConnector is a two-way routing layer for the home services industry. We route home services experts — restoration, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, construction, landscaping, cleaning, pest control — to the bids, projects and contracts their capability fits. And we route vendor recruitment firms — insurance panels, general contractors, facilities managers, municipal procurement, enterprise buyers, developers — to the qualified, compliant capacity their pipeline needs.

Who is this for?▾

Two sides. Home services experts who need live bids, projects and contracts — and vendor recruitment firms who hold those opportunities and need qualified supply. If you are a restoration firm looking for insurance panel work, a general contractor looking for compliant sub-trades, or an expert looking for a specific bid, this is for you.

How fast is routing?▾

For most core verticals, we identify the right counterparty within 24–48 hours of a complete brief. Complex mandates — large construction, multi-site facilities, public procurement — run on a longer cycle, typically measured in weeks. The commitment is the same: routing continues until fulfillment.

How are partners vetted?▾

Every partner in our network is screened for mandate fit, track record, licensing, insurance, capacity to execute and client-first delivery. We do not route to a marketplace. We route to a specific firm with the exact mandate. If the mandate does not fit, we do not make the introduction.

How do you get paid?▾

Three models. Fixed success fee upon fulfillment — agreed in advance, no result no fee, routing continued at no extra fee until fulfillment. Performance retainer — an ongoing routing mandate, credited against success fees, available on demand. Partnership — based on demand, negotiated per vertical. All fees are disclosed upfront.

Do you guarantee outcomes?▾

We guarantee the routing process: qualified demand and compliant capacity exist in every vertical we operate, and curated introductions continue until fulfillment. We do not guarantee the commercial outcome. All terms, diligence and decisions remain with the counterparty that engages with you. No funding, award, contract or commercial outcome is guaranteed.

What areas do you cover?▾

US nationwide. Our partner network covers all 50 states, with deepest coverage in Florida, Texas, Louisiana, California, New York and Georgia. Insurance panel, facilities and enterprise demand routes nationally. Municipal procurement routes by tender jurisdiction.

What do I need to submit?▾

A short brief. Who you are. What you need routed — a bid, a project, a contract, a qualified expert. Any standard documents you have. No ID, FICA or proof of address required to start a conversation. A complete brief gets a faster route.

Do I deal with HomeConnector or the partner?▾

You deal directly with the counterparty from the first introduction. HomeConnector makes the connection and then steps out of the way. All discussions, terms, diligence and delivery are between you and the specialist. The success fee is performance-based — earned when the connection produces a result.

How do I start?▾

Email routing@homeconnector.us with a short brief, or call the routing desk on 1-888-362-3731. No cost, no obligation. We will tell you plainly whether there is a qualified introduction to make — and route it if there is.
